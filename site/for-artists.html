Apply

Ask for an invitation

Gesso is invite-only. We add artists in small batches so each one gets real attention, starting with artists who already have buyers. Tell us about your work, where it sells today, and who sent you.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You're paid in dollars to your bank. Chips live on a public ledger, but they're store credit: nobody needs a wallet, and they can't be traded or cashed out.

Can I keep selling elsewhere?

Yes. Gesso is non-exclusive. Just mark a piece as sold here if it sells somewhere else.

Who sets the prices?

You do. We'll share what similar work sells for, but the number is yours.
