Rules

How the reserve stays healthy

12% of every sale

Originals, prints, objects, bookings and pledges all send 12% to the reserve before anyone else is paid.

Coverage stays above 1.0×

If the reserve ever drops below the value of all chips, the earn rate pauses at 5% until it recovers. No one's chips are cut.

Audited monthly

The reserve sits in a separate account. An outside accountant checks it every month and the report is posted here.
