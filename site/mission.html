Mission

Art should pay back the people who believe in it early.

Gesso is an art market owned by the people who collect it. Artists keep most of every sale. Collectors earn a stake in the artists they back, and get a say in where Gesso goes next.

Vision

A culture where backing an artist is an investment in your own life.

Art, food, music and experiences, all in one place, where every purchase or booking builds something for the person who made it and the person who paid for it.

Mission

Make collecting rewarding, and make the work last.

Pay artists 80%. Reward collectors with chips that rise as artists grow. Make everything physical in natural materials, and open spaces where people can see the work and spend their chips in person.

How Gesso is run

Four founding seats, and a vote for everyone who's in

The four founding members hold 51% of every vote. Everyone who has spent or pledged on Gesso shares the other 49%, weighted by what they've put in. Collectors also get first looks at new work and launches.
